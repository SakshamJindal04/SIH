About SafeKart

SafeKart is a robust product authenticity verification platform designed to combat counterfeiting and build trust between consumers and brands.

Who is it for?

This platform serves two primary groups: Consumers who want peace of mind knowing the products they buy are genuine, and Brands/Admins who need a reliable system to trace their inventory and protect their reputation.

Core Features

  • Instant Verification: Validate products instantly using cryptographic QR codes or manual barcode entry, preventing fake products from entering the supply chain.
  • Secure E-commerce: A mocked shopping flow that issues verifiable QR codes upon purchase, ensuring end-to-end traceability.
  • Admin Dashboard: Real-time logging of all verification attempts, allowing brands to monitor success rates and detect potential counterfeiting hotspots.
  • Dynamic UI: A modern, responsive interface featuring light/dark mode and seamless micro-interactions for a premium user experience.

Tech Stack

The SafeKart architecture is designed as a modern full-stack application:

HTML5 / CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript Node.js (Backend API) MongoDB (Database) RESTful APIs
